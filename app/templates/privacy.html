Privacy Policy & Data Protection Standards

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version 2.0 (Pilots in Indian Retail Pharmacies)

HealthSlip AI ("we", "us", or "our") operates as a secure digital prescription, clinical verification, and pharmacy management platform. We are committed to safeguarding personal health data in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable Pharmacy Practice Regulations in India.

1. Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, every patient whose data is processed through HealthSlip AI is recognized as a Data Principal with the following enforceable statutory rights:

  • Right to Access & Summary (Section 11): You have the right to obtain a summary of all health slips, active prescriptions, and personal identifiers processed by HealthSlip AI.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): You can request the correction of inaccurate medical demographics or exercise your "Right to be Forgotten" via our automated /api/patients/{id}/erase-data endpoint.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You may register grievances regarding data processing with our Data Protection Officer, who must respond within statutory timelines.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): In the event of death or incapacity, you have the right to nominate an individual to exercise rights on your behalf.

🔒 Patient Data Encryption at Rest & Safe Transit

All sensitive patient identifiers (names, mobile numbers, diagnosis notes) are encrypted at rest using AES-128-CBC authenticated Fernet encryption. External AI clinical assistance calls strictly strip all personally identifiable markers (names, phone numbers, postal addresses) before data processing.

2. Categories of Data Collected & Purpose

We process personal data solely for healthcare delivery purposes:

  • Clinical Demographics: Full Name, Age, Gender, Mobile Number for issuing verified prescriptions and medication reminders.
  • Prescription & Dispensing Records: Prescribed medications, dosage instructions, Jan Aushadhi generic substitutions, and dispensing counter audit logs (Drugs and Cosmetics Rules compliance).
  • Authentication & Verification Data: Salted HMAC-SHA256 hashed OTPs for secure patient access without passwords.

3. Audit Logging & Security Accountability

HealthSlip AI maintains an immutable tamper-evident Audit Log (audit_events) capturing all key actions including user authentication, OTP verification, prescription issuance, cryptographic sealing, pharmacy dispensing, and DPDP erasure requests.
